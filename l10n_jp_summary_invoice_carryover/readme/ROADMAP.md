- **Billings sharing customer, date and remit-to bank cannot be chained.**
  Neither is the other's previous billing, yet both count toward the
  outstanding balance, so **Payment Amount** can report a payment that was
  never received, and can go negative. Reached by one invoice per site or
  department (rolled up by commercial partner), or a same-day catch-up run.
  Workaround: set the amounts with the **Manual Adj.** toggles before
  validating.

- **Previous Billing is not re-pointed when other billings change.** Creating,
  back-dating or cancelling another billing leaves existing billings on a stale
  chain, understating their carryover. Validated billings are exempt by design.
